A reset starts fresh.

Below the on-call threshold (normally five minutes; nine in test mode), the first available person has 15 seconds to acknowledge. After acknowledging, they stay on duty until they pass, go off call, complete their extension, enter quiet hours, disconnect, or the campaign timer resets. Passing, missing the acknowledgment or a reset starts a passive 30-minute cooldown; “Go on call” clears it whenever you’re ready.

Only your own completion report marks your one extension as used. A timer reset may have come from someone outside this queue. Gamefound remains the source of truth, and participation cannot guarantee an extension.

No automatic penalties or removal for nonresponse. Only confirmed completion or leaving stops participation. Browser sleep and network interruptions can affect availability.
